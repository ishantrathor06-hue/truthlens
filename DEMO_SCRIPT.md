# 3-Minute Demo Script

**0:00–0:25 — Problem**
“Social media moves faster than fact-checkers. TruthLens is a neutral triage layer for newsrooms and citizen groups. It doesn't tell people what ideology to believe; it helps teams decide what needs attention.”

**0:25–1:00 — Public feed**
Show the dashboard, risk + recency ordering, category filters and clear status badges. Open one claim and point out the original claim, source, risk signals, evidence note and review history.

**1:00–1:35 — Submission**
Click “Submit claim”. Paste a sample viral claim, choose a platform and category, then add risk flags such as Breaking and Share before reading. Submit it and show that it appears as Unverified rather than being presented as fact.

**1:35–2:25 — Review workflow**
Open Review queue. Open the newly submitted claim. Explain that reviewers work from evidence, choose Verified / Disputed / Needs context, and that the original submission remains part of the audit trail.

**2:25–2:50 — Product decisions**
Briefly explain:
- Feed: risk + recency.
- Visibility: unverified claims are visible but clearly labelled.
- Editing: no silent edits; preserve history.

**2:50–3:00 — Finish**
Toggle dark mode as a quick polish moment, then return to the light theme. End on the public feed and say: “TruthLens turns a viral claim into a transparent, reviewable workflow.”
